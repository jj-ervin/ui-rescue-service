# AI UI Rescue Service — Scope v0.1

Status: **frozen pilot scope**

Prices: **UNVALIDATED pilot hypotheses**

## Service unit

One service unit is one agreed webpage or one reproducible UI state at one agreed URL, route, or local artifact. A materially different route, authenticated role, modal state, checkout step, dashboard view, or language variant is another unit unless it is explicitly included before approval.

The client and operator record the exact unit in [CLIENT-INTAKE.md](CLIENT-INTAKE.md). Ambiguity pauses work; it does not enlarge the unit.

## Offer A — One-page UI audit

**Pilot price: $79**

Included:

- review of the agreed page/state using supplied access and materials;
- baseline observations at up to three agreed viewport sizes;
- evidence-linked review of hierarchy, content clarity, typography, spacing/layout, color/contrast indicators, component consistency, responsive behavior, accessibility indicators, and trust/generic-design signals;
- 0–3/N/O scores using the rubric in [UI-AUDIT.md](UI-AUDIT.md);
- prioritized findings and a bounded improvement plan; and
- documented limitations and unverified items.

Not included:

- implementation, source edits, deployment, publishing, or ongoing monitoring;
- a comprehensive accessibility, security, performance, SEO, legal, privacy, analytics, or conversion-rate audit;
- user research, copywriting, branding, identity design, or new illustration; or
- additional pages or UI states.

## Offer B — One-page UI audit + fixes

**Pilot price: $249**

Includes everything in Offer A plus implementation of **up to five** client-approved, bounded visual fixes on the agreed page/state.

A bounded visual fix is one discrete change with a named target, proposed treatment, files/components expected to change, acceptance check, and rollback method. Examples include adjusting a page heading scale, correcting a spacing pattern, clarifying one primary action, improving a repeated component treatment, or repairing one responsive layout issue.

The following do not count as a single bounded fix and require a new scope decision:

- a full redesign, new page, new feature, or new design system;
- broad copy replacement or content strategy;
- framework migration, dependency/platform change, or infrastructure work;
- backend, database, authentication, payment, form-processing, analytics, or third-party integration work;
- changes spanning unlisted routes, roles, states, themes, or language variants; or
- an open-ended instruction such as “make the whole site better.”

If a proposed change exposes additional work, the approved fix stops at its written boundary. The new work is documented, not silently absorbed.

## Evidence and review boundary

The operator records:

- source URL or artifact and review date;
- page/state and viewport conditions;
- screenshots or other observable evidence when capture is authorized;
- exact findings and confidence/limitations;
- approved changes and affected files/components;
- checks performed, results, and checks not performed; and
- rollback or restoration instructions for implemented work.

A low score is not proof of business harm. A higher after-score is not proof of user preference, accessibility conformance, or revenue impact. Recommendations remain professional judgments until independently validated.

## Consent and access boundary

The client must confirm that they own or are authorized to provide the materials and access. Credentials, personal data, production secrets, and unrelated private material must not be copied into these templates.

Audit access does not authorize implementation. Implementation approval does not authorize deployment. Deployment or publication requires a separate, explicit written approval naming the target environment.

No work proceeds against a live production environment when an isolated copy, branch, preview, or staging surface is required but unavailable.

## Revision and communication boundary

The pilot includes one clarification round on the audit and, for Offer B, one review round covering the approved fixes. A request that changes the page, goals, approved list, or acceptance criteria is a scope change.

Delivery timing starts only after the required access, baseline, constraints, and written scope confirmation are available. External delays and client review time are recorded rather than treated as implementation time.

## v0.1 scope freeze

For v0.1:

- prices remain $79 and $249 and are labeled **UNVALIDATED**;
- the unit remains one page/state;
- Offer B remains limited to five approved bounded visual fixes;
- evidence, consent, isolation, matching captures, testing, limitations, and rollback remain required;
- deployment remains excluded unless separately authorized; and
- no recurring service, platform, automation, or additional product is implied.

Changing any item above requires a new version and an explicit decision. Do not revise the scope during an active engagement by editing completed approval records.
