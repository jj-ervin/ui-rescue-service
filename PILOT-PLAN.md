# UI Rescue service — Commercial pilot

Status: **proposed validation activity; not yet evidence of demand**

The immediate objective is the first paying customer for a bounded service, not scale, automation, or a larger product system.

## Offers and qualification

| Offer | Proposed pilot price | Qualified when |
|---|---:|---|
| Website UI Review | $79 | One public webpage/state has a clear audience and goal, desktop/mobile review is feasible, screenshot permission is clear, and the client accepts the exclusions |
| Website UI Rescue | $259 | The review qualifies, up to three small visual fixes can be explicitly approved, an isolated reversible implementation path exists, and production deployment is not implied |

Both prices and labor targets are hypotheses. Track actual time; do not promise the approximately 60-minute or three-to-four-hour internal budgets as guaranteed delivery times.

## Inclusions and exclusions

The controlling service boundary is [SERVICE-SCOPE.md](SERVICE-SCOPE.md). Reject or rescope requests for multiple pages, full redesigns, rebuilding, comprehensive accessibility or SEO audits, complex integrations, ongoing maintenance, open-ended implementation, or unauthorized production access.

## Prospect target and outreach

Pilot target: **15 qualified prospects**. Contacting any prospect is a separate, explicitly authorized activity; this document does not authorize automated outreach or collection of private information.

Respectful review-offer template:

> Hi [name] — I offer a small, fixed-scope review of one public webpage. The $79 pilot includes desktop/mobile inspection, up to five evidence-backed findings, practical recommendations, and one brief written follow-up. If that is relevant, I can send the exact scope. No obligation, and I will not continue contacting you if it is not useful.

Rescue-offer follow-up template:

> If you want help implementing a few findings, the separate $259 pilot covers up to three changes that you approve in writing, completed in an isolated and reversible environment where feasible. It does not include a redesign or unapproved production deployment.

Never imply prior review of a business when none occurred, manufacture urgency, promise outcomes, or repeatedly contact someone who declines or does not consent to follow-up.

Track at minimum:

- qualified prospects contacted out of the 15 target;
- replies and explicit interest;
- paid conversions by offer and price;
- actual labor, correction time, and material costs;
- scope failures, declined requests, and reasons; and
- privacy, access, permission, or delivery incidents.

The user's operating goal is **4–8 monthly engagements**, but the first gate is one real paying customer. Do not extrapolate monthly demand from interest, free reviews, fictional examples, or one sale.

## Stop/go criteria

After the 15 qualified-prospect pilot, or sooner if a safety or permission issue occurs, record one decision:

- **Go:** at least one paid engagement was delivered within the authorized boundary, the client accepted the process, and actual labor supports further bounded testing.
- **Adjust with explicit approval:** there is credible interest but repeated, specific friction in qualification, offer clarity, price, or effort; propose one bounded change and obtain approval before altering the scope.
- **Stop:** no paid conversion, repeated scope mismatch, labor makes the offers impractical, required access cannot be handled safely, or permission/privacy boundaries cannot be maintained.

Interest alone is not paid validation. A go decision authorizes only another explicitly bounded pilot step, not a marketing site, infrastructure, automated outreach, payments workflow, or expanded service.
